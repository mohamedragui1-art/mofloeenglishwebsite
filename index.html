What is MOFLOW English?

MOFLOW English is a structured online English-learning ecosystem designed to help learners build practical English skills through teacher-led sessions, guided practice, smart learning tools, AI-supported activities and progress tracking.

✦The teacher remains central. Technology supports the learning process.

Good questions

Before you
get started.

Here are a few things learners often want to know.

Is MOFLOW English online?

Yes. MOFLOW is an online learning experience that brings together teacher-led sessions and guided practice between sessions.

Who is MOFLOW for?

MOFLOW is for learners who want to use English in real life, including beginners, confidence builders and people learning for work, school or everyday situations.

What English level do I need?

Beginners and lower-level learners can start. The onboarding form helps us understand your current level, goals and learning needs.

Is MOFLOW just an English class?

No. MOFLOW combines teacher-led learning with guided practice, smart tools, AI-supported activities and progress tracking.

Does AI replace the teacher?

No. The teacher remains central. AI and digital tools provide additional practice and support between sessions.

What tools are included?

MOFLOW includes SpeakUp, WriteUp, VocabUp, learning trackers, curated resources and AI-supported learning activities.

Can I learn English for work or school?

Yes. You can share professional, school or study goals when you complete onboarding.

How do I join?

Complete the existing MOFLOW onboarding form to share your contact details and learning goal.
